    📧 Automatic Email Configuration (EmailJS)

    EmailJS lets you send emails directly from the browser — no server required. Complete the 3 steps below to enable automatic certificate notification emails.

    Step 1 — Create a free EmailJS account
    1. Go to emailjs.com → Sign Up (free)
    2. Under Email Services → Add New Service → choose Gmail / Outlook → connect your sender email arc@flame.edu.in
    3. Note down your Service ID (e.g. service_abc123)
    Step 2 — Create an Email Template

    Under Email Templates → Create New Template. Use these exact variable names in your template:

    To Email: {{to_email}}
    Subject: Your Certificate — {{program_name}} | FLAME Investment Lab

    Dear {{to_name}},

    We are pleased to inform you that your Certificate of Completion has been successfully generated.

    Programme: {{program_name}}
    Duration: {{duration}}
    Certificate Ref: {{cert_id}}
    Organisation: {{org_details}}
    Issue Date: {{issue_date}}

    Warm regards,
    Programme Coordinator
    FLAME Investment Lab, FLAME University Pune
    {{from_email}}

    Note down your Template ID (e.g. template_xyz789)

    Step 3 — Enter your credentials below

    Go to Account → API Keys to find your Public Key.
